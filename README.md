# EasyClip

A basic, lightweight audio distortion plugin built to add some of that *crunch* to your instruments. Without bells and whistles, clipping really can be easy!

<img width="397" height="293" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/eee4f0e3-fde5-4931-885d-e0b9666bf8c4" />

## Features
* **Simple Distortion Algorithm** -- No need for anything fancy when you just want hard clipping!
* **Clean Interface** -- Dial knobs quickly and without decision fatigue.
* **Low CPU Usage** -- Processing so light on your computer, even a feather weighs more!

## Controls
* **Drive** -- Controls the amount of gain pushed into the distortion stage.
* **Threshold** -- Cuts off samples at this dB, creating that crunch.
* **Out Gain** -- Boost or lower the volume of the sound after distortion.

## Tech Stack
* **Framework** -- JUCE<br>
* **Language** -- C++<br>
* **Plugin Format** -- Currently testing VST3, will test more soon.

## Future Roadmap
* **Oversampling** -- For those who want higher quality, a button will be provided to toggle oversampling.
* **Visualizer** -- See what is happening to the sound you are distorting!
* **UI Overhaul** -- Improve the look of the plugin without negatively affect performance.
